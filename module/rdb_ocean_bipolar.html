Pure corner-coordinate generator for the bipolar Arctic cap of a TRIPOLAR ocean grid (Murray 1996, J. Comput. Phys. 126, 251-273).

Ordinary lon-lat for lat <= phi_join; a conformal BIPOLAR cap for lat > phi_join. Two grid poles sit ON the join latitude at antipodal longitudes lon_pole and lon_pole+180, so the join circle is a coordinate line and the grid is C0-continuous across it.

Map: stereographic projection from the south pole (Z = tan((90-lat)/2)exp(ilon)), de-rotate by lon_pole, Mobius w = (Zp-A)/(Zp+A) with A = tan((90-phi_join)/2). Conformal coords (mu, xi) = (log|w|, arg w) are orthogonal by construction. The logical parameterisation makes the join a coordinate line and continuous with the lon-lat grid: i sets mu = log|tan((lam-lon_pole)/2)| (lon = lam on the join ring); j (s in [0,1]) rotates xi = sgn(pi/2 + spi/2) from the join (s=0) to the self-conjugate fold line (s=1). Inverse: w = exp(mu+ixi), Zp = A(1+w)/(1-w), Z = Zpexp(ilon_pole).

public pure subroutine bipolar_corner_latlon(lam_deg, s, phi_join, lon_pole, lat, lon)

Map a logical cap location to geographic (lat, lon) in degrees. lam_deg : pseudo-longitude (geographic lon at the join ring), any real (wrapped internally); the i-direction. s : cap-row fraction, 0 = join ring (lat = phi_join), 1 = fold line; the j-direction. phi_join: join latitude (deg); cap covers lat > phi_join. lon_pole: longitude (deg) of the first cap pole; partner at lon_pole + 180.
