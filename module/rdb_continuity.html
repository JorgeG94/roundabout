Holds scheme-variant flags and reusable workspace for the C-grid continuity-PPM (Lin & Rood) thickness-flux kernel, plus the public step routines that the split-explicit driver calls per barotropic substep. The kernel is the primary mass-flux producer for the ocean path: it consumes face velocities + cell-centred thickness and emits per-face mass fluxes that the tracer-advection kernels then reuse under CWC for free.

Phase 2b: method-of-lines PPM face reconstruction (Colella & Woodward 1984, eq 1.6 face value + eq 1.10 monotonic limiter) on the barotropic C-grid state. Closed-wall BC. Cells too close to a wall (< 2 cells from the boundary) fall back to first-order (h_L = h_R = h_centre); the resulting kernel preserves uniform fields bit-for-bit (the constancy-preservation property the lake-at-rest tests guard) and propagates Gaussian humps with < 5% peak diffusion over their own width.
