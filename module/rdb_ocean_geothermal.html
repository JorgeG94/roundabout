public subroutine ocean_geothermal_apply_tracers(grid, geo, ms, dt, active)

Add the geothermal bottom heat flux to the lowest massive tracer layer. Operates in hTr space (concentration thickness): d(hT_{k=1})/dt = Q_geo / (rho0 * cp) (units (W/m^2)/(kg/m^3 * J/kg/K) = Km/s, matching hTr).

private pure subroutine apply_geothermal_src_impl(hTr, budget, wet_mask, h_layer, src, nz, h_min)

Stamp src * wet_mask(i,j) onto the lowest massive layer of a tracer’s hTr array (first k with h_layer > h_min, scanning k = 1..nz from the bed up), mirror into the matching budget contributor. Flat-impl over plain allocatables — the outer subroutine reaches ms%tracers(idx)%hTr on the host before calling this.
