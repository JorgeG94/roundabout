Parent-state ingestion + interpolation state for nesting a regional ocean run inside a global/basin parent. Distinct from coastal BC_NESTED: parent state on a coarser mesh (ingest + remap onto our vertical grid), tides composed in via rdb_ocean_tides, mandatory T/S clamp, and a Flow-Relaxation-Scheme (FRS) zone of nrelax cells rather than a hard clamp. Scaffold (kernels not yet implemented).
