GPU-resident periodic ghost-wrap helpers. Seam invariant after every wrap call: (a) every ghost cell equals its interior partner, and (b) u(i_w, ·) == u(i_e, ·) bit-for-bit (i_w = nghost+1, i_e = nghost+nx_phys+1).

Explicit-shape dummies (never assumed-shape), j-outer / i-inner do concurrent for NVHPC GPU coalescing. The fast barotropic substep does NOT call these — it wraps inline in its !$acc kernels async(1) region.

public subroutine ocean_periodic_wrap_centre_3d(fld, nx_total, ny_total, nz, nx_phys, ny_phys, nghost, wrap_x, wrap_y, no_wait)

Fill ghost cells of a cell-centred 3D field (e.g. h_layer, hTr, T, S). no_wait (optional, default .false.): when .true., loops issue on OpenACC queue 1 and the routine returns WITHOUT syncing, so a batched caller can pipeline many tiny ghost-slab wraps and !$acc wait(1) once. Default ⇒ self-contained blocking wrap. Not pure (directives).
