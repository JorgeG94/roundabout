Freshwater density (kg/m³) the ISOMIP+ melt-rate CONVENTION divides by — Asay-Davis et al. (2016), GMD 9, 2471–2497, §3.3: melt rates are reported in m/yr of ICE-EQUIVALENT freshwater. public subroutine fill_umo(state_handle, buf)

3-D layer mass transport through the zonal (u) face, kg/s — MOM6/CMOR umo. Reads ms%mass_flux_x_layer, the SAME per-layer volume flux (u · h_face · dy_cu, m^3/s) continuity differences to advance h_layer each step — not a re-derived cell-centre reconstruction (cf. transport_x, which averages the two bounding faces onto the T-cell for a depth-integrated VELOCITY diagnostic). umo = rho_0 · mass_flux_x_layer (Boussinesq: mass flux through a face is the reference density times the volume flux). A STAGE flux: mass_flux_x_layer is overwritten by every rdb_continuity call, so this reports whatever the LAST continuity call of the step left there, not a time-mean over the step’s sub-stages. See fill_mass_transport_x_impl for the face/T-cell index convention (also where the dropped eastmost boundary face is).
