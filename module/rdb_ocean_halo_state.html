public subroutine ocean_halo_exchange_centre_field(fld, grid, bc, device_resident)

Seam ghosts of ONE cell-centred 2-D forcing field a file reader filled on physical cells only: a two-pass centre exchange (MPI, or the periodic wrap on a single rank) plus the tripolar north fold. Users: the SSS-restoring target (its deposit kernel runs over the full array, so a stale ghost would make the seam tile disagree with its neighbour), the chlorophyll field, the data-override Q_heat/Q_salt writes (components off), and the assembled Q_heat/Q_salt/sw_net that KPP/EPBL read in the ghost columns (ocean_seam_refresh_surface_flux, ocean_seam_refresh_sw_net). Single-rank non-periodic: a no-op; requires ocean_halo_init.

public subroutine ocean_halo_exchange_ice_transport(ice, grid, bc, device_resident)

X4 of the sea-ice MPI plan: the seam ghosts every advective substep of ice_transport_step reads — the cell-averaged category masses mca_ice/mca_snow (the PPM donors, 5-point stencil) and the riding intensive tracers m_ice, enth_ice, sal_ice, enth_snow (the PCM donors). mca_* ghosts are zeroed by the IST->CAS conversion and the ride/mass updates leave the ghost band one substep old, so this runs at the top of EVERY substep. On one rank with a periodic axis the primitives wrap. Also carries the tripolar north fold of the same six fields (plain-copy scalar contract) — without it the fold-seam row’s advective stencil read a stale/unrelated mirror cell every substep, which is how ice piled up without bound on that row.

Seam ghosts of sw_net = q_sw + sw_ice, formed over the full array from a q_sw the bulk consumer wrote on physical cells only. Read in the ghost columns by the KPP shortwave coupling and the EPBL penetrating-SW TKE ledger. Call right after ocean_surface_flux_refresh_sw_net; a no-op unless that routine ran (use_components .and. sw_from_qsw).

private subroutine ice_halo_centre_flat(fld, nxt, nyt, nz, device_resident)

Explicit-shape seam: a contiguous ice array of any rank (0:ncat third bound, rank-4 category x layer) is handed in by sequence association and exchanged as one (nxt, nyt, nz) centre field. The generic ocean_halo_centre resolves on the DUMMY’s rank, so the rank-4 actuals cannot call it directly.
