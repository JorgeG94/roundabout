Reproducibility. The solar-position sin/cos are safe_sin/safe_cos (host, rdb_safe_math) and rdb_sm_sin/rdb_sm_cos/rdb_sm_atan2 (the per-cell zenith kernel), so cosz does not depend on a cell’s vector position or tile width. Every phase is bounded (day-of-year angle in [0, 2 pi), hour angle within a few hundred degrees), so no time-growing argument reaches the library.

Builds the nine-field atmospheric state tas, huss, uas, vas, psl, rlds, rsds, prra, prsn (Tsujino et al. 2018, JRA55-do) on the model T-grid: time-blend each field’s current bracket on the shared PR-14 reader into a reusable longitude-circle x latitude-band WINDOW buffer, then regrid that window onto the physical interior with the in-model lat-lon weights (rdb_regrid_latlon, Jones 1999). No MPI communication in the provider itself — every rank reads its own hyperslab (the reader’s win_i0/win_j0/win_nx/win_ny override, C2c-1) and regrids independently.

rsds – held interval + diurnal cosine-zenith factor (sw_interp = "coszen", the namelist default, C2c-2b). The JRA55- do rsds file carries 3-hour interval MEANS stamped at the interval CENTRE (01:30, 04:30, …), record k meaning the mean over the UTC bucket [3k h, 3k h + 3 h). Registering it with hold = .true. and t_offset = sw_offset_s (default +5400 s, half the bucket) makes the shared reader return, for model time t, the record whose stamp is the last one <= t + 5400 s: exactly the record of the bucket that CONTAINS t, PIECEWISE-CONSTANT (rsds_rec, blend weight forced to 0). (The offset is added to the QUERY time; a negative one selects the PREVIOUS bucket’s record – it did until 2026-11, and divided it by the current bucket’s cosine mean, which blows up at every sunset/sunrise bucket boundary.)

The diurnal shape within the bucket is restored by rsds = rsds_rec * cosz / cosz_mean, where, with the Sun’s declination and the equation of time frozen at their value at the bucket midpoint (Spencer 1971 Fourier fit, host-side rdb_calendar arithmetic) and omega the hour angle, cos Z(omega) = a + b cos omega with a = sin(lat) sin(dec) and b = cos(lat) cos(dec): * cosz_mean is the EXACT mean of max(0, cos Z) over the UTC bucket [t_rec, t_rec + 3 h) that rsds_rec represents; * cosz is the EXACT mean of max(0, cos Z) over the model step [t, t + dt) that the flux is applied for (not the instantaneous value at t: a step that straddles sunset must carry the step’s mean insolation, not the value at its first instant). Both are differences of ONE closed-form antiderivative of max(0, a + b cos omega) (clipped at the sunrise/sunset hour angle acos(-a/b); polar day and polar night are the unclipped / empty cases), so they telescope: for steps that tile the bucket (dt divides 3 h, t on the dt grid) Sum_steps rsds * dt = rsds_rec * 3 h to round-off in EVERY cell, however thin the sunlit sliver; and since each step’s integral cannot exceed the bucket’s, rsds <= rsds_rec * 3 h / dt (6 x the record at dt = 1800), exactly 0 when the Sun is down for the whole step, and 0 in a bucket whose mean cosine is below 1e-9 (a sliver of about 1 s; the record there is ~1e-6 W/m2 and the 0/0 would otherwise be noise).

The window is the interval the flux is APPLIED over. With dt_therm_ratio = N > 1 the surface flux sampled on a thermo step is applied over N*dt, not dt: diabatic_first samples at the interval start and applies over [t, t + N dt); diabatic_first = .false. samples on the interval’s last step and applies over [t - (N-1) dt, t + dt). atm_sw_window is the ONE place that says so (the engine and the tests both call it) and atm_state_update integrates over exactly that window. Windows are never split across a bucket edge: the bucket length must be a multiple of the window length and the window start must sit on the window-length grid of the UTC clock (start_date time of day included); anything else FAILS LOUD (atm_sw_coszen_refresh) rather than biasing the energy. Bucket length is not a constant: atm_sw_check_axis derives it at configure from the rsds record spacing (must divide 86400 s, the records must be uniform and stamped at the interval CENTRE) and refuses a sw_offset_s that is not half of it (a namelist still carrying -5400 would silently reproduce the old one-bucket-late record). The reader is given sw_offset_s + 0.5 s so the record switch never rides a float-equality tie on the dt grid. The solar-position scalars are computed HOST-SIDE (calendar arithmetic is not device-safe); the per-cell device kernel does only elementwise trigonometry on geolonT/geolatT. Time of day is UTC (JRA55-do’s convention), taken from start_date + t, so a start_date that is not on a 3 h boundary is handled. orb_year is the reserved Berger (1978) orbital-parameter follow-up – validate_config refuses any value other than its default, since this chunk’s declination uses the FIXED Spencer coefficients. sw_interp = "linear" disables all of the above (hold = .false., t_offset = 0, plain blend, same answer as every other flux) – rsds is then rsds_rec copied straight through.

There is NO bulk-flux/ice-seam CONSUMER here (that is C2c-3) – the engine only configures this slot and refreshes it every step; nothing reads tas/huss/… yet.

Two reusable window buffers, not one per field. win_bl/win_cv are each sized to ONE weight set’s source footprint (regrid_window) and reused by every field that uses that weight set: tas, huss, uas, vas, psl always use the bilinear set (wb, &ocean_atm_forcing_nml states_regrid has no other legal value); rlds, rsds, prra, prsn use wb or the conservative set (wc) together, selected once at configure by fluxes_regrid. Both weight sets are always built (bit-identical either way the knob is set) – only the per-field BINDING differs. Each field’s call sequence is blend-into-window, regrid-into-a-physical-size scratch (tgt_bl/tgt_cv, a persistent workspace – never a per-step local-allocate, CLAUDE.md “Persistent kernel workspaces”), then scatter that scratch into the field’s own ghosted array at the nghost offset – so the window/scratch buffers are safely reused read-after-write within one field’s update, before the next field overwrites them.

Ghosts are untouched. Every field array is (nx_total, ny_total) but only the physical interior is written here – no consumer in this chunk reads a ghost cell, and extrapolating into one is the data-forcing anti-pattern rdb_ocean_data_forcing’s docstring already warns against.

private pure function atm_cosz_cumulative(x, a, b) result(c)

Antiderivative C(x) = Integral_0^x max(0, a + b cos w) dw of the clamped cosine of the zenith angle, b >= 0, up to an additive constant that cancels in every difference. Three cases: a >= b (polar day: never clamped, C = a x + b sin x); a + b <= 0 (polar night: C = 0); else the Sun is up for |w mod 2 pi| < w0, cos w0 = -a/b, and C adds the per-day integral P = 2 (a w0 + b sin w0) once per elapsed day. Device-callable (routine seq, same-module).

public subroutine atm_state_update(this, reader, metrics, t, dt, win_shift, win_len)

Blend + regrid every one of the nine fields’ current bracket, then (sw_interp = "coszen") restore rsds’s diurnal shape from the held bucket mean – see the module docstring’s rsds paragraph. t must be the same model time ocean_data_input_update_all was just called with – the reader enforces this and aborts otherwise (see ocean_data_input_update_2d’s fail-loud freshness check). dt is the model step; the coszen window is [t, t + dt) unless the caller passes win_shift/win_len (from atm_sw_window: the interval a thermo-cadence flux is really applied over). Unused under sw_interp = "linear". metrics supplies geolonT/geolatT for the coszen kernel (unused, and not read, when sw_interp = "linear").

public pure subroutine atm_sw_window(dt, therm_ratio, diabatic_first, thermo_step, shift, length)

The interval [t + shift, t + shift + length) over which the shortwave sampled at model time t is APPLIED. A step that is not a thermo step (or therm_ratio <= 1): [t, t + dt). Thermo step at therm_ratio = N > 1: the surface flux is applied over N*dt – [t, t + N dt) under diabatic_first (sampled at the interval start), [t - (N-1) dt, t + dt) otherwise (sampled on the interval’s last step). The engine and the tests both call this.

private pure subroutine atm_coszen_apply_impl(nx_total, ny_total, nghost, nx_phys, ny_phys, hour_frac_rec, sdec, cdec, eqtime, t_off, dt, bucket, geolonT, geolatT, rsds_rec, cosz, cosz_mean, rsds)

The ONE device kernel. Per physical T-cell, with cos Z(omega) = a + b cos omega (a = sin(lat) sin(dec), b = cos(lat) cos(dec), omega the hour angle, advancing ATM_EARTH_OMEGA rad/s): the exact mean of max(0, cos Z) over the step [t, t+dt) (cosz) and over the whole bucket (cosz_mean) – both differences of atm_cosz_cumulative – and rsds = rsds_rec * cosz / cosz_mean (see the module docstring for the conservation and bound this gives). t_off = seconds from the bucket start to t; hour_frac_rec = UTC hour of day at the bucket start; sdec/cdec/eqtime frozen at the bucket midpoint. Explicit-shape throughout; no cross-module calls – every quantity but geolonT/geolatT is a host-precomputed scalar.
