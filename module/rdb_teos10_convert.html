Pure TEOS-10 conversion primitives (no EOS state, no namelist knobs).

Salinity-scale and temperature-scale converters between the “model” variables this tree has used historically (practical salinity SP, potential temperature PT) and the TEOS-10 variables (Reference/Absolute Salinity SA/SR, Conservative Temperature CT), plus the TEOS-10 polynomial freezing point.

Refs: IOC/SCOR/IAPSO (2010), “The international thermodynamic equation of seawater - 2010 (TEOS-10)”, IOC Manuals and Guides No. 56 (the “TEOS-10 manual”); McDougall & Barker (2011), “Getting started with TEOS-10 and the Gibbs Seawater (GSW) Oceanographic Toolbox”, SCOR/IAPSO WG127 (the “GSW toolbox” paper, which the pt_from_ct Newton-McDougall iteration and the ct_from_pt surface polynomial are from).

OM3 parity override (no delta-SA atlas): this tree sets SA == SR (Reference Salinity from practical salinity alone, no spatial Absolute-Salinity anomaly atlas) – the same simplification the production EOS_VARIANT_ROQUET_* branches already make. Every routine below that takes an “sa” argument therefore expects SR (or, under the cons_abs convention a later PR adds, the model’s SA tracer taken as-is); there is no separate sa_from_sp.

All point routines are pure elemental and !$acc routine seq – parameters only, no allocation, fixed iteration counts – so they are device-callable from inside a do concurrent kernel in this library. The explicit-shape 1-D *_impl kernels below wrap them for callers outside the library (a test executable’s own kernel cannot resolve a library routine seq symbol at device link).

WIRED into rdb_eos: roquet_spv_point / roquet_rho_point call ct_from_pt_derivs here for their PT->CT conversion + analytic dCT/dPT, dCT/dSA (chain-ruled to the model (PT, SP) at the call site) instead of carrying an inline copy of the surface polynomial. Both rdb_eos branches previously carried their OWN independently transcribed copy that MISPLACED two terms (the degree-6/7-in-sqrt(SA) coefficients 123.59576582457964 and -48.5891069025409, which belong in the pure-salinity, y^0 Horner coefficient, landed on the y^1/y^2 coefficients instead) – 8.1e-3 degC of CT error at S=35, theta=10 degC, and up to 2.3e-2 degC CT / 4.5e-3 kg/m^3 of density over SP 0-42, theta -2..32 degC, p 0-5000 dbar (both Roquet variants) – that the two rdb_eos copies agreed with each other on because both carried the identical mistake, which is why no regression test caught it until checked against the real GSW gsw_ct_from_pt. Fixed by wiring both branches through this verified module.

public pure elemental function ct_freezing_poly(sa, p, sat_frac) result(ct_f)

Conservative Temperature at the freezing point of seawater (TEOS-10 manual §3.33; McDougall & Barker 2011; GSW gsw_ct_freezing_poly) – polynomial fit, not the exact Newton-Raphson chemical-potential solve. p is SEA PRESSURE IN DBAR (the TEOS-10/GSW convention this routine and its coefficients are fit in – NOT the Pa convention rdb_eos’s p_ref uses; a caller crossing that boundary must convert). sat_frac is the saturation fraction of dissolved air in seawater (0 = no dissolved air, 1 = fully air-saturated).

public pure elemental function pt_from_ct(sa, ct) result(pt)

Potential temperature (p_ref=0) from Conservative Temperature – the modified Newton-Raphson (“Newton-McDougall”) method, 1.5 iterations fixed (McDougall & Barker 2011; GSW gsw_pt_from_ct): a rational initial guess, one full Newton step with the cheap analytic dpt/dct from the guess itself, then one more step using the expensive-but-exact dpt/dct = -cp0/((pt+T0)*gibbs_tt) evaluated at the midpoint. Fixed iteration count, no data-dependent loop – device-callable.

public pure elemental subroutine ct_from_pt_derivs(sa, pt, ct, dct_dpt, dct_dsa)

CT (bit-identical to ct_from_pt) plus its ANALYTIC derivatives dct_dpt = dCT/dPT and dct_dsa = dCT/dSA, obtained by regrouping the SAME surface polynomial ct_from_pt evaluates (McDougall & Barker 2011; GSW gsw_ct_from_pt) into explicit coefficients of y = 0.025PT, c_k(x) (x = sqrt(CT_SFACSA)), each itself a short polynomial in x – so dCT/dPT is a plain Horner-in-y derivative and dCT/dSA follows the chain rule through dCT/dx and dx/dSA = CT_SFAC/(2x). Regrouping is exact (no re-derivation of the published nesting, just re-association of terms already present in it) – verified against ct_from_pt to < 5e-11 and against centred finite differences of ct_from_pt to <= 1e-7 relative (tests/test_ocean_teos10_convert.F90).
