Tracer diffusivity generated by bottom drag: the mean-flow kinetic energy the bottom stress removes in the viscous bottom boundary layer (BBL) is partly spent on mixing the water just above the bed. A law-of-the-wall profile sets the diffusivity, an energy ledger limits it, and the result is ADDED to the other interior diffusivities (tracer diffusivity only: the BBL viscosity is already carried by kv_bbl in the vertical-friction glue).

Pipeline: 1. bbl_mixing_set_tke (once per outer step, right after the per-face viscous BBL is refreshed): per face, the friction velocity u*_f = kv_bbl_f/(sqrt(c_d) bbl_thick_f) and the BBL-mean ALONG-FACE velocity ubar_f (thickness-weighted over bbl_thick_f, signed component). Per cell: u*_h (half the area-weighted sum of the four faces’ u*_f^2 over A_T), the BBL mean-KE loss P_bbl = sqrt(c_d) Sum_f (A_f/A_T) u*_f ubar_f^2 (all four faces summed, no one-half) and |f|_h, the mean of |f| at the four corners. 2. bbl_mixing_compute (thermo cadence): a column sweep up from the bed over the interior interfaces. The available power effic * P_bbl decays upward, exp(-h/lambda) per layer with 1/lambda = max(1/max_decay, |f|/u*_h); the wall-law diffusivity Kd = kappa u*^2 z (D-z) / (u* D + |f| z (D-z)) (z the height above the bed, D the live column depth) is cut so that Kd dz max(N^2, N^2_min) never exceeds the power left, and the power it uses is removed. With no stratification to pay for (N^2 = 0 and no rotation floor) the diffusivity fills to the cap kd_max while any power remains. 3. bbl_mixing_merge_into_kt (every stage): kt += kd_int on the interior interfaces.

The wall law is the classical Kd = kappa u* z (von Karman 1941; Prandtl mixing length) closed at the surface by (1 - z/D) and capped by the rotational Ekman-layer scale u*/|f| (Zilitinkevich-type rotation limit of the neutral boundary layer, L = kappa u*/|f|; see also Killworth & Edwards 1999, J. Phys. Oceanogr. 29, for the rotation-limited BBL thickness the face bbl_thick already carries). The energy ledger follows the turbulent-kinetic-energy budget of a stratified boundary layer (Osborn 1980, J. Phys. Oceanogr. 10: mixing efficiency; the buoyancy flux Kd N^2 is paid out of the shear production), with the Joule-loss decay of the available power with height above the bed. N^2_min = Omega^2 (use_omega) is the same rotation floor the internal-tide closure uses.

Interface convention (shared with rdb_ocean_tidal_mixing): kd_int(:,:,K) lives at the bottom interface of layer K, global bottom-up (k=1 bed, k=nz surface); K = kbed (the bed) and K = nz+1 (the free surface) are exactly 0. The interface stratification is the SAME layer-centred N^2 producer as the tidal kernel (column_layer_n2).

Default off (enable = .false.): no state is allocated and the pipeline is bit-identical.

public pure subroutine bbl_column_sweep(nz, kbed, h, n2, ustar, f_abs, tke_col, max_decay, use_omega, omega2, von_karman, kd_max, kd)

One column’s law-of-the-wall BBL diffusivity with the decaying energy ledger (see the module header). Bottom-up: h(k) the layer thicknesses, n2(k) the stratification at the TOP interface of layer k (interface k+1), kd(K) the output at interface K (zero at K <= kbed and K = nz+1). tke_col is the power offered at the bed (m^3/s^3). D - z is evaluated as the live column depth minus the height of the interface above the bed (equal to the distance down from the surface up to round-off). kd_max enters only the unstratified branch; the stratified branch is the wall law scaled by used/need.
