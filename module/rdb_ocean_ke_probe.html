Rebuild of the transient per-term KE attribution meter used to localise the split-corrector anti-damping term (LAGRANGIAN_PGF_BUG.md §R). Samples the h-weighted layer kinetic energy between the split stage’s tendency applies and prints KE_ATTR rows: the dKE between consecutive samples IS the energy injected/removed by the segment just executed, so one instrumented run names the anti-damping term directly.

Three regions per sample, one small device pass each: full — all physical cells; rim — cells within rim_band of any GLOBAL domain wall (the slow exponential is rim-trapped); east — cells within east_band of the GLOBAL east wall (the mode-1 internal Kelvin wave is wall-trapped there).

Multi-rank: every region is defined in GLOBAL cell indices (i_offset_global/nx_global). The KE is summed PER OWNED FACE (see ke_region_one): each u/v face is read by exactly one rank, so NO u/v ghost is ever read – on a PERIODIC axis the east (north) wrap slot is a copy of face 1 that the full-range applies advance on their own, so it is not read either; face 1 then also belongs to cell nx_g (ny_g) through the wrap. The only ghosts the probe relies on are those of h_layer (the face thickness straddles a seam, and at a periodic face 1 it needs cell nx_g), current at both sample points (the entry sample follows the stage-entry exchange; at stage close h_layer was last set by the continuity chain, which ends in a halo exchange and periodic wrap). Caveat: under eulerian_z + ssp_rk2 the vertical advection updates ghost thickness locally AFTER that exchange, so the seam-face thickness at the stage-close sample is layout-dependent there (known defect, fixed separately). Tripolar fold: the v row at jg = ny_g+1 (the fold line, owned by the north rank row) is read AS STORED, like the one-rank run; whether it is refreshed before the stage-close sample is not verified. Sums are reproducing EFP sums, combined with halo_allreduce_efp_list (collective: all ranks call whenever the step-window gate is open) and printed once by rank 0, so the digits are identical on any rank count.

Gated by &ocean_debug_nml ke_attr with an optional [ke_attr_start_step, ke_attr_end_step] outer-step window. Default off ⇒ bit-identical (untaken branches only); model state is never written. ke_probe_coradv_split (KE_ATTR_PV rows) has NO caller in the stepping loop today (it is only imported there); it is decomposition-safe as a routine but additionally needs the Coriolis scratch (ke_centre) ghosts to be current when called.

public subroutine ke_regions_efp_local(h, u, v, nx, ny, nz, nghost, nx_phys, ny_phys, i_off, j_off, nx_g, ny_g, rim_w, east_w, periodic_x, periodic_y, tot)

RANK-LOCAL EFP partial sums of the h-weighted layer KE over the three regions (tot(1:3) = full, rim, east); the caller combines them across ranks. Per cell: KE = ¼(h_W·u_W² + h_E·u_E²) + ¼(h_S·v_S² + h_N·v_N²) with h_face the centred face thickness (area factor dropped; attribution only needs a consistent measure). Regions are tested on GLOBAL cell indices (i_off/j_off = the tile’s global offsets, nx_g/ny_g the global extents), so they are decomposition-independent.

private subroutine coradv_one(h, u, v, fx, fy, kec, idxCu, idyCv, nx, ny, nz, nghost, nx_phys, ny_phys, i_off, j_off, nx_g, ny_g, rim_w, east_w, dt, periodic_x, periodic_y, part, region, total)

One (part: 1 PV / 2 KE, region: 1 full / 2 rim / 3 east) EFP sum of coradv_split_efp_local; seven accumulators per k-slab. Reads only OWNED faces (u west faces ig=2..nx_g of its cells, v south faces jg=2..ny_g; ig=1 / jg=1 too on a periodic axis, with the region from the wrapped cell nx_g / ny_g) and, at a seam, the h and ke_centre ghost of the neighbouring cell – kec is the Coriolis kernel’s scratch, current only if the caller samples right after it (this routine has no caller in the stepping loop today).

private subroutine ke_region_one(h, u, v, nx, ny, nz, nghost, nx_phys, ny_phys, i_off, j_off, nx_g, ny_g, rim_w, east_w, periodic_x, periodic_y, region, total)

One region (1 full, 2 rim, 3 east) of ke_regions_efp_local, summed PER OWNED FACE. The per-cell KE ¼(h_W u_W² + h_E u_E²) + ¼(h_S v_S² + h_N v_N²) is regrouped as Σ_faces ¼·h_face·u_face²·n, n = number of the face’s two adjacent cells that lie in the region (0..2), so the global sum is algebraically the per-cell one. A face is read only by the rank that owns it: west faces (global index 1..nx_g) of its cells, plus the global east boundary face (ig = nx_g+1) on the east-most rank; v likewise with south faces / the global north face. No u/v GHOST is ever read (they are stale at the stage-close sample). PERIODIC axis (periodic_x/periodic_y, the GLOBAL edge tags): the east (north) boundary face slot is a wrap copy of face 1, advanced on its own between exchanges, so it is NOT read; face 1 is the east face of cell nx_g (ny_g), so the region test wraps the non-existent cell 0 to nx_g and face 1 gets its weight from cells nx_g and 1. Only owned prognostic faces are read. h ghosts are read (the face thickness straddles a seam; at a periodic face 1 it needs the h of cell nx_g, the west-most rank’s west ghost, a wrap/halo copy that is current for the same reason as every other h ghost): h is current at both sample points – the stage-entry sample follows the stage-entry exchange, and at stage close h_layer was last updated by the continuity chain, which ends in a halo exchange + periodic wrap (ssp_rk2), or not at all before the sample (pred_corr runs continuity after it). Not so under eulerian_z + ssp_rk2: the vertical advection then updates ghost thickness locally after that exchange, so the seam-face thickness is layout-dependent there (known defect, fixed separately). Tripolar fold: the v row jg = ny_g+1 is the fold line, read as stored (refresh before the stage-close sample not verified). One k-slab at a time, seven EFP accumulators, carried between slabs.
