Consumer of the shared NetCDF reader that turns a monthly surface chlorophyll climatology into the per-column, per-band shortwave opacities ocean_surface_flux_t%sw_kappa (Manizza et al. 2005, GRL 32, L05603):

kappa_n = a_n + b_n * chl**p_n [m-1], uniform in depth,

land columns carry sw_opacity_land. The band split (sw_band_fractions) and the N-band transmission (sw_transmission_nb) live in rdb_ocean_surface_flux, so the deposition kernel, the KPP B_0 coupling and the EPBL SW ledger all read one definition.

Two-call contract, as rdb_ocean_data_forcing: sw_chl_configure runs once in the configure phase BEFORE ocean_state_enter_data (registration opens the file and allocates the reader brackets); sw_chl_apply runs once per outer step right after ocean_data_input_update_all(t). The chlorophyll destination (sf%sw_chl_field) and the opacity array are mapped by the surface-flux slot, so this slot adds no enter_data term.

Time axis. The climatology is read DATA_TIME_CYCLIC with a 365-day period; model t = 0 is taken as the start of the file’s year (day 0), so the first record (mid-January) is reached at about day 14. Floor. Chlorophyll is floored at SW_CHL_FLOOR before the power so land / fill values (negative) and zeros are defined.
