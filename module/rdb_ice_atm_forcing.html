Fills ocean_sea_ice_t’s coupleable seam (atm_sf0/atm_dsfdt/atm_sw_dn/atm_fprec) either from the &ocean_ice_nml scalar restoring knobs (ice_atm_forcing_restoring) or from the prescribed atmospheric state (ice_atm_forcing_from_atm, C2c-3b) – the engine dispatches on atm_state_t%enable. SF(T) is always upward-positive, linearised at a surface temperature T: SF(T) = sf_0 + dsf_dt*T. The seam is identical whether this filler, the atm-driven one, or a live coupler fills it – that is what makes the model coupleable (PLAN_ICE_PR3c S”coupleable seam”; PLAN_PR26_snowfall.md S13 fixes atm_fprec’s shape/units/lifecycle for PR-55 to fill from data; PLAN_C2c.md S4 “Ice seam”).

ice_atm_forcing_restoring (v1, unchanged). SCALAR/UNIFORM: SF(T) = lambda*(T - T_air) => sf_0 = -lambda*T_air, dsf_dt = lambda, sw_dn = sw_down (uniform), atm_fprec = snowfall (uniform). Ghost rows are filled too (harmless – ice_thermo_columns only steps physical cells). Default-off byte-identity: never called unless ice%enable.

ice_atm_forcing_from_atm (C2c-3b). A linearisation of the surface energy balance at T_s = 0 degC (BULK_T0_KELVIN) using Large & Yeager’s (2004, 2009) over-ice constant transfer coefficients (C_D = C_H = C_E = 1.63e-3 – no Monin-Obukhov stability iteration, unlike the ocean’s bulk_flux_column; this is the simpler CORE/OMIP “sea-ice” bulk formula, not the open-water one): with rho the ambient air density (ideal-gas law off psl/ tas/huss, same form bulk_flux_column uses) and wv = max(|(uas,vas)|, BULK_WIND_MIN) the atmospheric wind speed (no ice velocity subtracted – same “no ice-specific bulk drag law” posture as tau_a_x/y, D7 in rdb_ice_state):

sf_0 = epssigmaT0^4 - rlds + rhocpC_Hwv(T0 - tas) + rhoL_sC_Ewv(qsat_ice(T0) - huss) dsf_dt = 4epssigmaT0^3 + rhocpC_Hwv + rhoL_sC_Ewvdqsat_ice_dT(T0)

(eps is the atm-forcing bulk-flux emissivity knob, &ocean_bulk_flux_nml emissivity – LY09’s over-ice formula does not distinguish an ocean- from an ice-emissivity, so this reuses the one knob rather than adding a second; qsat_ice is bulk_flux_qsat_ice, L_s is BULK_LATENT_SUBLIMATION). atm_sw_dn = rsds, atm_fprec = prsn – same fields the ocean consumer reads, same “ghosts untouched” contract as atm_state_t itself (its own docstring: no consumer reads a ghost cell; a ghost tas = 0 would divide rho by zero). Unlike the restoring filler, this kernel therefore loops the PHYSICAL interior only (the atm%nghost offset idiom atm_state_scatter_impl/ bulk_forcing_core_impl already use) – ghost cells of the four seam arrays simply keep whatever the restoring filler (if ever called earlier) or the source=0.0_wp allocation left there, which is harmless by the same “ice_thermo_columns only steps physical cells” contract.

public pure subroutine ice_atm_forcing_from_atm(ice, atm, cfg)

Outer shim: forward the four seam arrays, the nine-field atm state’s own cached geometry (atm%nx_phys/ny_phys/nghost/ nx_total/ny_total) and the bulk-flux emissivity knob to the device kernel. A no-op unless atm%enable (mirrors every other atm-provider consumer’s own guard, even though the engine dispatcher only calls this when it already holds).
