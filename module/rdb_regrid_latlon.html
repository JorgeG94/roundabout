Grid-agnostic regridding library: builds bilinear or first-order conservative weights from a regular lat-lon SOURCE grid onto the model’s T-grid, stored in an ELL (padded-stencil) layout, and applies them with one device kernel. No namelist knob, no engine wiring here – bit-identity by construction; the engine-side selector knobs (states_regrid/fluxes_regrid/…) are added by a later PR.

References: Jones, P. W. (1999), “First- and Second-Order Conservative Remapping Schemes for Grids in Spherical Coordinates”, Monthly Weather Review, 127, 2204-2210; Sutherland, I. E. and Hodgman, G. W. (1974), “Reentrant Polygon Clipping”, Communications of the ACM, 17(1), 32-42; Murray, R. J. (1996), “Explicit Generation of Orthogonal Grids for Ocean Models”, Journal of Computational Physics, 126, 251-273.

Type Visibility Attributes Name Initial
integer, public, parameter :: REGRID_NMAX_CAP = 64

Fail-loud ceiling on the per-cell stencil size nmax: a 4-degree target on a TL319-like (~0.5625-degree) source needs ~64 source cells inside one conservative polygon’s bounding box; a 1-degree target needs 9-12. Exceeding it is always ierr, never a silent truncation of the accumulated area.
real(kind=wp), private, parameter :: POLE_TOL_DEG = 1.0e-9_wp

A target corner within this of +-90 deg is treated as AT the pole.
integer, private, parameter :: REGRID_POLY_MAXV = 24

Max vertex count carried through the Sutherland-Hodgman clip (quad or the 7-vertex pole-cap polygon, clipped by one rectangle: at most 7+4 = 11 vertices in practice).
