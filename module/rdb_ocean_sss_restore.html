Surface salinity restoring toward a time-varying 2-D target field (surface_flux%sss_target, blended from &ocean_restore_nml sss_file by ocean_data_forcing_apply), in the salt-flux form used by climatological ocean models (Griffies et al. 2009, “Coordinated Ocean-ice Reference Experiments (COREs)”, Ocean Modelling 26, sec. 4.1; the net-zero adjustment is the CORE-II salinity-restoring practice). Per wet cell, with S the surface-layer salinity:

  1. dS = clamp(S_tgt - S, +-max_delta)
  2. F = rho0 * w * dS * open (w = piston velocity, m/s)
  3. global net removal (optional), over PHYSICAL cells, weighted by the cell area A: scaling: P = sum A max(F,0), N = sum A min(F,0); if P > 0 > N the larger-magnitude side is scaled by min(P,-N)/max(P,-N) so sum A F = 0 and no cell changes sign; mean: F <- F - (sum A F / sum A open) * open.
  4. deposit d(hS_top) = (dt/rho0) F into the first live layer (k_top) and mirror it into salt_budget_surface, exactly as apply_surface_src_2d_impl does for Q_salt (so the closed salt budget sees it as a source).

Units. F is in the model’s Q_salt convention, the one dS/dt = Q_salt/(rho0 h) is written in: salinity in g/kg times kg/m^2/s. In SI salt mass that is 1e-3 * F [kg salt m^-2 s^-1]; the 1e3 is absorbed because the tracer is carried in g/kg. The resulting tendency is the piston law dS/dt = w dS / h, identical to the scalar-target path.

Why F is never stored. F is a pure function of the live surface salinity, the target, and scalars, so the kernels recompute it in each of the (at most) three passes - the two global sums and the deposit. That avoids a persistent workspace (whose lifecycle and intent(in) access through the dynamical core’s sf dummy would both be awkward) at the price of one extra trivially cheap evaluation per thermo step.

Decomposition independence. P, N and the open area are accumulated in EFP fixed point (Hallberg & Adcroft 2014) and combined across ranks in ONE halo_allreduce_efp_list, so the scaling factor is bit-identical for any rank count. The target’s seam ghosts are exchanged by the caller, so the full-array deposit matches the neighbour tile.

A cell whose target is non-finite (a masked file value) or whose open fraction is zero takes no part: F = 0 there and it is excluded from every sum. mem:separate: every array is owned and mapped by ms / sf / metrics.
