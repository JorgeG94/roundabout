private subroutine ice_halo_centre_flat(fld, nxt, nyt, nz, device_resident)

Explicit-shape seam: a contiguous ice array of any rank (0:ncat third bound, rank-4 category x layer) is handed in by sequence association and exchanged as one (nxt, nyt, nz) centre field. The generic ocean_halo_centre resolves on the DUMMY’s rank, so the rank-4 actuals cannot call it directly.
