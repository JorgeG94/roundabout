bt_wide_entry_exchange

private subroutine bt_wide_entry_exchange(this)

Fills the wide ghost band before the fast loop with ONE packed exchange (eta, ubt, vbt, rem_u, force_u, rem_v, force_v). H_ref is exchanged once, at the first call. The previous-substep velocities are not exchanged: the fast loop re-seeds them from ubt/vbt over the whole wide array when bebt > 0 and never reads them otherwise (their coefficient is 0). Under porous barriers / closed faces the two per-solve BT widths (w_dy_cu, w_dx_cv) follow in one wide face exchange each (the packed set is full at three x / y arrays). Counter effect: +1 bt_packed per call (+1 centre_2d on the first; +1 face_x_2d, +1 face_y_2d more when the widths are refreshed). Dispatches to the non-polymorphic _impl body.

      !! Dispatches to the non-polymorphic `_impl` body.
