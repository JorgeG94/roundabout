bt_wide_entry_exchange

private subroutine bt_wide_entry_exchange(this)

One wide grouped exchange (eta+ubt+vbt) + wide singles for the other 7 input arrays (H_ref, ubt_prev, rem_u, force_u, vbt_prev, rem_v, force_v). Fills the entire wide ghost band before the fast loop. Counter effect: +1 bt_group, +1 centre_2d, +3 face_x_2d, +3 face_y_2d. Dispatches to the non-polymorphic _impl body.
