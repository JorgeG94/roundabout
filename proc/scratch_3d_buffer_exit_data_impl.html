public subroutine scratch_3d_buffer_exit_data_impl(this)

Release the scratch payload from the device. No copyout — scratch contents are per-step intermediates with no host-side meaning. No-op when never allocated (mirrors scratch_3d_buffer_enter_data_impl’s gate).
