ocean_restart_t

type, public :: ocean_restart_t

Restart-manager lifecycle marker. The registry is built per-run by ocean_state_build_restart_registry (it holds live pointers into the freshly allocated slots), not stored here. Cadence is driven by the driver off cfg%restart_interval/cfg%output_dir.
