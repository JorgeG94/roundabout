logical, public :: bound_kh = .false.

MOM6 BOUND_KH analogue for the velocity-Laplacian paths (scalar nu_h and the flow-aware per-face closure). When .true., the per-face harmonic viscosity is clamped to bound_coef·0.125/(dt·(idx²+idy²)) — ~0.25× the forward- Euler stability limit, matching MOM6’s Kh_Max_xx margin. Load-bearing beyond simple FE stability: the barotropic mode receives the depth-mean viscous force FROZEN over the outer step (via F_bt), and for grid-scale gravity modes with ω·dt ≳ 1 an unbounded λ·dt = ν·k²·dt ≳ 0.6 frozen force is applied with reversed phase — ANTI-damping — which exponentially pumps rim-trapped barotropic modes through the Δu corrector (the 600² Lagrangian double-gyre h-guard blow-up; e-fold ~10 outer steps). The clamp keeps λ·dt ≤ 0.5·bound_coef at every face so the corrector loop stays damped at any resolution. Default .false. ⇒ bit-identical.
logical, public :: bound_thin_layer = .false.

Stress-tensor path only. Thin-layer treatment of the Griffies & Hallberg (2000) stress form: (a) the CFL bound on the Laplacian coefficient is scaled by r = min(1, h_min/(h+eps)) at T and Bu (h_min = the smallest surrounding face thickness), (b) the corner thickness is the harmonic-type mean that vanishes when ANY one of its four face thicknesses does (instead of the arithmetic 4-point mean), (c) face thicknesses are masked by the T wet mask. Default .false. ⇒ bit-identical.

Coastal lateral BC selector (shared with the lateral-mix / Coriolis kernels). .false. (free-slip) masks the corner shear stress by wet_q; .true. Constant biharmonic horizontal viscosity (m^4/s). procedure, public, non_overridable :: build_biharm_stress => ocean_hvisc_build_biharm_stress

  • private subroutine ocean_hvisc_build_biharm_stress(this, metrics, f_corner, ah_vel_scale, smag_bi_const, smag_on, cor_on, cor_vel)

    Host-side, once at configure and BEFORE enter_data: build the 2-D statics of the stress-form biharmonic (biharm_stress) at the T centres and the Bu corners: floor max(AH, c_A·Delta^3) (AH = this%nu_4) Smag C_b·Delta^4 (0 unless smag_on) Coriolis Delta^6·f/(5·V^2) (0 unless smag_on .and. cor_on) stencil 1/D4, the discrete forward-Euler limit of the biharmonic stress divergence (Griffies & Hallberg 2000): Ah_max = bound_coef·0.5/(dt·D4); D4 = 32/Delta^4 on a uniform square grid, so Ah_max = c_b·Delta^4/(64·dt). Delta^2 = 2·dx^2·dy^2/(dx^2+dy^2). D4 is the larger of the u-row and v-row absolute coefficient sums of div(Ah·strain(L(u))), L the stress-form Laplacian; cells whose stencil leaves the interior copy the nearest fully interior value.
