budget_contributor_t

type, public :: budget_contributor_t

One source/sink of a conserved quantity, populated by a physics kernel each step (adds dt·delta into per_cell) and drained at eval cadence (drain_contributors integrates per_cell·mask·dA over k into total_integrated, then zeroes per_cell). total_integrated is the cumulative RHS checked against LHS = current_total − values_init.
