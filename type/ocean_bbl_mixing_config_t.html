ocean_bbl_mixing_config_t

type, public :: ocean_bbl_mixing_config_t

Law-of-the-wall bottom-boundary-layer diapycnal mixing (&ocean_bbl_mixing_nml). The mean-KE the bottom stress removes in the viscous BBL, effic * P_bbl, mixes the water above the bed with a wall-law diffusivity kappa u*^2 z (D-z)/(u* D + |f| z (D-z)) bounded by an upward-decaying energy ledger; ADDED to the tracer diffusivity only. Needs the per-face viscous BBL (&ocean_vdiff_nml bbl_glue + a bottom drag with hbbl > 0) and thermodynamics. Default off => bit-identical. Knob table: docs/generated_nml_knobs.md.
