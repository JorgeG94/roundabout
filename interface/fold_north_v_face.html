private pure subroutine fold_north_v_face_2d(v, nx_total, ny_face, nx_phys, ny_phys, nghost, negate)

2D y-face (Cv) fold: north-halo fill + on-line antisymmetric projection at the fold row. Used for the barotropic bt_vbt field in the BT fast loop. negate (default .true., see the u-face twin) selects true-vector (sign flip + self-conjugate zero) vs. scalar (copy + self-conjugate left unchanged, matching fold_north_corner_2d’s scalar contract) — a scalar on a v-face (e.g. visc_rem_v) is the SAME physical attribute of the SAME face seen from both sides of the seam, so the duplicated DOF at the fold line must agree, not cancel.
