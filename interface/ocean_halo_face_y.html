private subroutine ocean_halo_face_y_3d(fld, nz, device_resident)

Two-pass X-then-Y halo exchange for a 3D y-face field. Batched: packs ALL nz layers into one buffer per direction. Face-y ownership (D1): south rank sends (ng+1)nxtnz northward, north rank sends ngnxtnz southward. X-pass uses standard centre-style (ng per column per layer over nyt1 rows).
